Kai.AI Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated October 9, 2026

This is a starter privacy document and must be checked against actual data practices and reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

1. Information collected

Kai.AI may process account email, authentication identifiers, profile information you choose to provide, prompts and content you submit, generated outputs, support requests, reports, and basic technical or usage data needed to operate and protect the service.

2. Terms acceptance record

When you sign up, the current implementation records a terms acceptance flag, terms version, acceptance timestamp, and privacy-policy version in signup metadata. Metadata alone is not a tamper-proof legal audit record; stronger server-side recording is still needed.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide requested features, authenticate accounts, respond to support requests, improve reliability, prevent abuse, process transactions through configured providers, and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers configured to host the application, authenticate users, deliver email, provide AI features, or process payments. The final published policy must name the actual providers and accurately describe what data each receives.

5. Content and AI providers

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, private keys, or sensitive personal information into prompts. Before connecting an AI provider, explain what prompt and file data is sent to it and review that provider's terms and retention settings.

6. Retention and security

Data should be retained only as long as necessary for stated purposes or legal requirements. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Passwords should be handled by the authentication provider and never stored as raw text by Kai.AI.

7. Your choices

Provide a clear process for requesting access, correction, deletion, or privacy help before public launch. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to prevent fraud.

8. Children and age requirements

Age restrictions and parental-consent processes must be clearly defined and implemented before launch. Do not collect children's information contrary to applicable law.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The final policy must identify the responsible operator and a working privacy contact channel before launch.
